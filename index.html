Planning tools

Interactive versions of the methods I apply when planning a sprint.

Figures shown are illustrative examples.

Capacity forecast
How the forecast is calculated
  1. Look at recent delivery. The forecast uses the number of tickets the team completed in each of the last four sprints.
  2. Weight recent sprints more. The most recent sprint counts four times, then three, two and one, because recent performance best reflects the current team.
  3. Adjust for leave. The weighted average is reduced in proportion to planned leave across the 10 working days of the sprint.

Forecast team capacity for the next sprint

Recent sprints are weighted more heavily, and the forecast is adjusted for planned leave.
